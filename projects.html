A selection of completed projects across Serbia — cinemas, offices, cafes, and commercial spaces.

Cineplexx Beo Shopping

Cinema Fit-Out Soundproofing Plasterboard Client: Studio M

Full dry construction and soundproofing fit-out for the Cineplexx multiplex cinema at Beo Shopping Centre. Works included multi-layer acoustic partition walls, suspended ceilings, mineral wool insulation, and high-spec surface finishing across all cinema halls.

Cineplexx Galerija

Cinema Fit-Out 20 Installers ~80% Cinema Works Client: Studio M

Large-scale cinema fit-out at Galerija Shopping Centre — one of our biggest deployments with 20 installers on site simultaneously. Approximately 80% of all cinema dry construction and finishing works, including acoustic walls, ceilings, insulation, and decorative surfaces.

Bosch Airport City

Office Fit-Out 2 Full Floors Client: Studio M

Complete interior fit-out of two full floors for Bosch at Airport City Business Park. Works covered all dry construction phases: metal framing, plasterboard systems, suspended ceilings, plaster finishing, and painting across open-plan and partitioned office areas.

HTEC, Milutina Milankovića

Office Fit-Out 7th & 8th Floor 16 Workers On-Site Client: Studio M

Fit-out of the 7th and 8th floors for HTEC Group's Belgrade office on Milutina Milankovića. A high-spec office interior delivered with 16 workers on site, covering all dry construction, suspended ceilings, detailed plaster finishing, and painting.

Sidek Business Premises

Interior Fit-Out Drywall Decorative Painting

Interior fit-out for Sidek business premises including plasterboard partitions, surface finishing, and high-quality decorative painting works including Oikos Travertino Romano decorative plaster-paint system.

Soljica Caffe
