Our Services

Professional dry construction and interior finishing — from structure to final surface.

Soundproofing

We design and install complete acoustic insulation systems for cinemas, recording studios, offices, hotels, and residential buildings. Our soundproofing solutions use high-density mineral wool, acoustic batts, resilient channels, and multi-layer gypsum board configurations to achieve specified acoustic ratings.

We have direct experience in large-scale cinema soundproofing — including both Cineplexx Belgrade locations — where acoustic performance is critical.

Mineral Wool Acoustic Batts Multi-Layer Drywall Resilient Channels Cinema Systems

Plasterboard Installation

We install metal stud and drywall systems for partition walls, suspended ceilings, cladding, and complex architectural shapes. Our systems include single and double-layer configurations, fire-rated assemblies, moisture-resistant boards for wet areas, and bespoke curved structures.

We work with leading brands to ensure every installation meets structural, fire, and acoustic performance requirements.

Metal Stud Framing Partition Walls Suspended Ceilings Fire-Rated Systems Moisture-Resistant Board

Renovations

We manage complete interior renovations — from demolition and layout reconfiguration to full fit-out and handover. Our renovation services cover offices, commercial premises, cafes, restaurants, and residential apartments, handling all dry construction, surface finishing, and painting stages.

Whether refurbishing a single floor or an entire building, our teams deliver on schedule and to the specified standard.

Office Renovation Commercial Fit-Out Layout Changes Residential Refurb Turnkey Delivery

Painting Works

We provide professional interior and exterior painting services — from surface preparation and priming through to standard and premium decorative finishes. Our team is experienced in applying high-end systems including Oikos Travertino Romano, Van and other decorative plaster-paint solutions.

We ensure thorough surface preparation on every job, giving the final coat the adhesion and durability it requires.

Interior Painting Exterior Painting Oikos Travertino Romano Decorative Finishes Surface Preparation

Plaster Works

We apply machine and hand-finished plaster systems to walls and ceilings with exceptional consistency and surface quality. Our plasterers work with gypsum, cement, and lime-based materials for smooth, textured, and decorative finishes across all project types.

We also provide specialist fire-rated and water-resistant plaster systems for technically demanding environments, ensuring full compliance with project specifications.

Machine Plaster Hand Finish Skim Coat Decorative Plaster Fire-Rated Systems

Our goal is to be not just a contractor, but a reliable partner — one that helps clients deliver their projects on time, with precision and confidence.
