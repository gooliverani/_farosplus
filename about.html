About Us

A trusted partner in dry construction and interior finishing — based in Belgrade, Serbia.

Who We Are

Faros Plus is a professional construction finishing company based in Belgrade, Serbia. We specialize in dry construction systems, plasterboard installation, soundproofing, plaster works, renovations, and painting works.

Founded on a commitment to quality and precision, we operate on projects throughout Serbia — from large-scale commercial and industrial developments to high-end office interiors and residential fit-outs.

Every project we take on is approached with rigorous planning, skilled execution, and a focus on delivering results that meet and exceed client expectations.

What We Do

We work across all phases of interior construction — from raw structure to finished surfaces. Our teams are experienced in metal stud framing, drywall systems, machine and hand plaster, acoustic and thermal insulation, suspended ceilings, painting, and final decorative finishes.

We collaborate directly with architects, general contractors, and project owners to ensure seamless coordination and on-time delivery. Our portfolio includes landmark projects such as Cineplexx cinemas, Bosch Airport City, HTEC offices, and Belgrade Waterfront.
