About Faros Plus

Faros Plus is a professional construction finishing company based in Belgrade, Serbia. We specialize in dry construction, plasterboard installation, soundproofing, plaster works, renovations, and painting — serving commercial, industrial, and residential clients.

With years of hands-on experience, our teams execute every project with precision — meeting deadlines without compromising quality.
